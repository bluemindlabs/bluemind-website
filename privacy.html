Privacy policy.

This page explains what personal data this website collects, why, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as we can.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Bluemind, which is building an environmental intelligence layer for the ocean. In this policy, “Bluemind”, “we” and “us” mean the team responsible for this site and the data controller for any personal data it collects.

This policy covers this website only. Our community app and intelligence interface are separate services and may have their own terms.

What we collect

When you use the contact form. We receive the name, organisation, email address and message you enter. Organisation is optional; the other fields are required so we can reply.

When you visit the site. Like any website, the servers that deliver these pages see your IP address and basic technical details such as your browser type. This is needed to send the page to you.

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies on this site, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

How we use it

  • To read and reply to messages you send us through the contact form.
  • To keep a record of conversations about research, partnerships and collaboration.
  • To deliver the website and keep it secure.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your contact details for marketing unless you ask us to keep you updated.

Legal basis

Where data protection law such as the GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running this website, and, where you ask us to do something before entering into an agreement with you, on taking steps at your request.

Services we use

A small number of third parties process data on our behalf so the site can work:

  • Formspree receives contact form submissions and forwards them to us. See Formspree’s privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on this page, which means your browser requests them from Google and Google receives your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Our hosting provider delivers the pages and may keep short-lived server logs.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, they rely on recognised safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep contact messages for as long as the conversation is active and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or we need to keep them for a legal reason.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • receive a copy of it in a portable format.

To delete your account and data, use our deletion request form. For any other request, contact us as described below. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, use the contact form and mention “privacy” in your message.
