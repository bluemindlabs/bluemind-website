Your data

Delete your account and data.

Use this form to ask us to delete your Bluemind account and the personal data we hold about you. You don’t need to give a reason.

What happens next

  1. We receive your request and reply to the email address you give us to confirm it’s really you. Please send the request from the address linked to your account.
  2. Once confirmed, we delete your account and the personal data linked to it, including your profile, contributions tied to your identity and past messages with us.
  3. We email you when it’s done. We aim to finish within 30 days.

Good to know

  • Deleting your account can’t be undone.
  • Observations you shared publicly may stay in the dataset in anonymised form, with nothing that identifies you.
  • We may keep a small record that you asked for deletion, and anything the law requires us to keep.

Read more about how we handle data in our privacy policy.
